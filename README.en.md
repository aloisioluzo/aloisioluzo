## 👋 My story

> **I started in technology by solving problems. Over time, I realized that I also wanted to build the solutions.**

For more than **10 years**, my professional journey has been connected to technology.

I built my experience in **technical support, systems, infrastructure, and corporate environments**, working every day with users, applications, and problem solving.

But web development has always been part of my story.

Throughout this journey, I have worked with **HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, WordPress, MySQL, SEO, and website development**. At one point, my career moved more strongly toward support — an experience that gave me a practical view of real-world problems and the people who use technology.

Today, I am reconnecting with that side of **building**.

My goal is to combine my support experience with **Front-End, automation, and Artificial Intelligence**, turning ideas and real needs into digital solutions.

### 🧠 How I think

**Entender → Planejar → Construir → Testar → Melhorar**

I do not want to simply know tools. I want to use them to build things that make sense.
